# Research result reporting and Writee handoff — on-demand guide

Researchee produces **rigorous mathematical research output**, not an academic manuscript. Answer the original mathematical problem first, with precise assumptions, constructions, theorem statements and complete essential proofs. Mathematical LaTeX notation is welcome; journal-oriented prose, Introduction, Related Work, publisher formatting, polished manuscript sections, BibTeX preparation, priority/novelty audits, and submission packaging belong to Writee.

## Research outcome

Use **Complete Solution**, **Partial Progress**, or **Unresolved Investigation** with exact scope. State every hypothesis and logical quantifier as needed, give genuinely proved results and necessary proof details, and label conjectural, conditional, computational and unverified literature claims. Do not compress a difficult step into an unsupported assertion. Keep references traceable for **mathematical use**. Do not imply the result is new or unreported based on a limited research literature search.

## Research Continuation Record (when unresolved)

Append a self-contained concise record: original goal/assumptions; established lemmas, exact constructions and reproducible data; specific unresolved gap; route status and rigorously established obstructions; prioritized next mathematical actions; necessary sources with verification limits. Do not create a narrative research diary. Omit on full solution unless requested.

## Writee interface

When asked to turn results into a paper or when the user requests an export for Writee, provide a **Research Handoff** following `writee-handoff.md`. Include the original task, proved claims and proofs, dependencies, mathematical status, open gaps, computational evidence and research references with verified-vs-unverified provenance. Never imply that Writee was invoked, or that cross-plugin transfer is automatic, without actually using a supported interface. Do not substitute this record for Writee's bibliography and prior-art checks.

## Explicit LaTeX request

If the user explicitly asks Researchee to express a theorem or proof as `.tex`, provide faithful research-source LaTeX when file tools are available, without turning the task into a full journal manuscript. Prefer handing complete editorial/multi-section paper requests to Writee. The existing `assets/research-report-template.tex` remains a legacy optional mathematical report skeleton, not a default publication template.
